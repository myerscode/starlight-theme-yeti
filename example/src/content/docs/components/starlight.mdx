Starlight's built-in content components keep working as normal. The theme restyles them so they sit alongside its own chrome: the same corner radius, hairline borders and accent colour as the rest of the site.

## Steps

<Steps>

1. Install the theme and its peer dependencies.

2. Register `starlightThemeYeti()` in your Starlight plugins.

3. Pick a `palette` and point `changelog` at your `CHANGELOG.md`.

</Steps>
